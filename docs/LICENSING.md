# SRSL-1.1 implementation and migration notes

Current SWORD-owned KEYSTONE material is offered under [SRSL-1.1](../LICENSE). This is a **source-available licence**, not OSI open source. Previous SRSL-1.0 copies continue under their original valid grants. The former complete text is archived at [LICENSES/SRSL-1.0.txt](../LICENSES/SRSL-1.0.txt).

The permitted non-commercial purposes have an explicit list. For-profit internal use (including evaluations and cost savings), commercial research and commercial services need a separately executed agreement. Research and personal users do not need registration or first-run acknowledgements.

The licence now explicitly reserves model-training rights subject to mandatory exceptions, clarifies termination and infringement remedies, and reserves claims in competent foreign IP forums. **It does not automatically impose 50% profit participation on infringers**; that percentage remains an opening proposal for negotiated commercial agreements. Model outputs are not automatically covered merely because a model saw source material.

A future effective commercial entitlement or technical protection measure can be separately evaluated. **No activation system, execution gate, telemetry or DRM is implemented by this licensing change.** Merely putting an anti-circumvention sentence in a licence does not create a protected TPM.

Before broader rollout: confirm each source file's copyright ownership; review included third-party licences; preserve older release tags; obtain UK IP/contract lawyer review; and evaluate US copyright registration timing, publication history and eligibility separately. US statutory damages/fees and UK additional damages are conditional, not automatic. This document is not a legal opinion.
